SELECTION-BIAS DISCLOSURE, IN THE HEADLINE. The CROSS-ARM rule flags only arms SLOWER than their reference, so it is a one-sided filter, and it acts in opposite directions: new-cell references are gen1 (dropping slow gen2 arms flatters gen2 and inflates the ratio); the old cell's reference is a single arm (dropping slow old arms flatters old and deflates the ratio). The net sign is not known in advance. So the report ALWAYS also gives: S1 = every arm that is VALID, OUTLIER-free, not contended, right generation, CROSS-ARM flags IGNORED; S2 = every VALID right-generation arm with no other exclusion (descriptive); and the number of arms each rule dropped. If PRIMARY and S1 disagree about whether the interval excludes 1.0 or 1.032, that disagreement is the result and no single figure is reported.
