WHAT WAS KNOWN WHEN THIS WAS WRITTEN. Exactly one arm's verdict: task1e-0-old-on-U (VALID; CONTENDED "contended" -- nimbus_beacon_node 101% on core 39, reth-binary 76% on core 47, htop 60-68% on cores 35 and 37, all sampled on arm cores 32-63; one CROSS-ARM TTFT flag on session_3, 33.8 s vs the single reference arm's 30.6 s). Arm 1 (new) had reached engine_ready and had no verdict, no per-session number, no result of any kind; arms 2-5 had not started. Nothing about the size or sign of the old/new ratio was known. This amendment is made on the strength of the contention pattern in arm 0's boundary samples and on a structural argument, not on any outcome.

THE TENSION BEING FIXED. The registered design protects against contention with the STATIONARITY test (non-stationary contention => UNRESOLVED). That implies contention that is stationary across the series is tolerable: a paired, strictly interleaved ratio needs contention to be EQUAL across the two cells, not absent. But the inherited per-arm rule discards any arm marked CONTENDED. That rule was written when contention was the exception. With reth-binary, op-reth, nimbus_beacon_node and other users' jobs running and (as arm 0 shows) landing on arm cores whatever their nominal affinity, it discards every arm, so no series could ever return a result however evenly the contention fell. Left as written the two rules make the experiment unconditionally unresolvable.

THE SUPERSEDING RULE.
1. CONTENDED ("contended" or "unknown") ALONE NO LONGER DISQUALIFIES AN ARM. Justification: the quantity of interest is a ratio between strictly interleaved cells, and a paired ratio is robust to contention common to both cells. Absolute levels stay uncitable and are always labelled contended.
2. Every other inherited exclusion is UNCHANGED: not VALID, any OUTLIER note, any CROSS-ARM flag, wrong GENERATION label each still disqualify an arm. Those indicate a disturbance that hit one arm or one session, which a paired design cannot absorb.
3. The stationarity tests registered in task1e-PREDICTIONS.txt (load1 spread/cell-gap/trend; foreign-CPU spread/cell-gap/trend; >=50% foreign sightings on an arm core differing by >=2 between cells) are now load-bearing and are applied EXACTLY as written, over every arm run. If they trip the answer is UNRESOLVED and the ratio is printed as "not a result", however good it looks.
4. BOTH RESULTS ARE REPORTED, in the headline: the STRICT result under the original rules and the RELAXED result under this rule, each with the number of arms it admitted and dropped. If they disagree that is stated. The original verdict is not erased. The RELAXED set is now the primary; STRICT is reported beside it. The S1 sensitivity (CROSS-ARM ignored) is reported in both forms: S1 as originally defined (CONTENDED still disqualifies) and S1r (CONTENDED and CROSS-ARM both ignored; still VALID, OUTLIER-free, right generation), plus S2, with drop counts, and the fitted position coefficient for every set.
5. REPLACEMENT BUDGET narrowed at team-lead's direction: it is spent ONLY on an arm disqualified by an OUTLIER note or a CROSS-ARM flag (or INVALID), and only if the deficit is reachable within the original cap of 2 extra arms per cell, strictly alternating, with the position rule and companion rule of the original. A CONTENDED-only arm is never replaced.

Foreign processes are NOT staying on their nominal affinities: reth-binary was described as pinned to 30-50 and was sampled on core 47; nimbus_beacon_node on cores 39 and 52; htop on 35 and 37 (arm 0's boundary samples, "top_other_cpu" cpu_num).
