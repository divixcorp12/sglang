THE ADDENDUM. CROSS-ARM is computed TWO ways at analysis time:
 (i) as registered: against the historical reference set in clean-reference.json (for the old cell that is a single arm from another night, task1c-1);
 (ii) LEAVE-ONE-OUT WITHIN THIS SERIES: once the arms exist, each arm's sessions are compared with the median of the OTHER arms of the SAME CELL in THIS series, by the verdict's own rule and threshold (8%): decode tok/s slower than 0.92 x the median for any session; TTFT more than 1.08 x the median for sessions 1..n (session-0 TTFT is exempt, as in the verdict).
(ii) IS THE PRIMARY FOR DISQUALIFICATION; (i) is reported alongside, with its own drop count. This is not a relaxation: the threshold, the disqualifying effect and the OUTLIER rule are unchanged; a point reference from a different, differently loaded night is replaced by a reference distribution from the same hour, which the rule needed and could not have before the series existed.
Details fixed now: the reference arms for (ii) are all OTHER arms of the cell that are VALID (no further filter, so a disturbed sibling can shift the median; with 3 arms per cell each arm has 2 references and the median of two is their mean, a weak reference, and that is stated in the report); an arm with no other VALID arm in its cell is "not judged" and is not flagged by (ii); replacement arms, if any, join the reference set. The set produced with (ii) plus the amendment's CONTENDED relaxation is called RELAXED-LOO and is the primary; RELAXED-HIST (same with (i)), STRICT, S1, S1r and S2 are reported beside it, each with its drop count, position fit and the two verdict lines. If the old cell still has fewer than 3 clean arms under (ii), the answer is UNRESOLVED and is reported as such.
